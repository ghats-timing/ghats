\chapter{Window Functions}
\label{app:window-functions}

Here are listed the functional forms of the available window functions.  The
functions refer to the time interval \([-T/2,T/2]\) and are zero outside it.
The parameters \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), \(\sigma\), and \(\gamma\) must be
supplied by the user for the windows that require them.

In the implementation, \routine{ghats_window} uses the dimensionless variable
\[
  u = t/T ,
  \qquad -1/2 \leq u < 1/2 .
\]

\begin{longtable}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.22\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.46\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
Window & Functional form & Parameter \\
\midrule
\endhead
Boxcar &
\[
  f(u) = 1
\]
& none \\

Bartlett &
\[
  f(u) = 1 - \left|\frac{u}{1/2}\right|
\]
& none \\

Hann &
\[
  f(u) = \frac{1}{2}\left[1+\cos(2\pi u)\right]
\]
& none \\

Welch &
\[
  f(u) = 1 - \left(\frac{u}{1/2}\right)^2
\]
& none \\

Cosine &
\[
  f(u) = \cos(\pi u)
\]
& none \\

Hanning &
\[
  f(u) = \cos^2(\pi u)
\]
& none \\

Hamming &
\[
  f(u) = \alpha + (1-\alpha)\cos^2(\pi u)
\]
& \(\alpha\) \\

Triplet &
\[
  f(u) = \exp\left(-\frac{\beta}{|u|}\right)\cos^2(\pi u)
\]
& \(\beta\) \\

Gauss &
\[
  f(u) = \exp\left[-\frac{1}{2}\frac{u^2}{\sigma^2}\right]
\]
& \(\sigma\) \\

Kaiser &
\[
  f(u) = \frac{I_0\!\left(\gamma\sqrt{1-(2u)^2}\right)}{I_0(\gamma)}
\]
& \(\gamma\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The numerical implementation is in \routine{ghats_window}.  The default
window is \texttt{Boxcar}.  The windows \texttt{Hamming}, \texttt{Triplet},
\texttt{Gauss}, and \texttt{Kaiser} require \param{WPAR}.
